## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s initiative sync: when a PM creates an initiative in Notion, Juno creates the matching Linear and Zentrik initiatives and reports what’s missing.
- **Value proposition:** Keep every initiative set up to the team standard across Notion, Linear, and Zentrik without the PM standardizing it by hand, and tell the PM exactly what’s still missing.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consistency (deal-breaker) | Do the initiative name and linked records match across Notion, Linear, and Zentrik, with no duplicates? | Yes / No | Any name differs, any initiative is duplicated, or the project link is missing. | n/a | Names are identical in all three tools, exactly one initiative exists per tool, and the Linear project is tied to the Linear initiative. |
| Faithfulness (deal-breaker) | Is everything Juno created or reported traceable to a real record, with nothing invented? | Yes / No | Any milestone, interview count, or status appears that doesn’t exist in a source. | n/a | Every milestone, status, and claim in the output can be traced to a source record you can open. |
| Gap Flagging | Does Juno clearly name what is missing against the team standard (Intent Canvas, milestones, Pulse, Zentrik interviews, Fireflies transcript)? | 1 to 5 | Missing items are not mentioned, or Juno says everything is complete when it isn’t. | Most missing items are named, but one is missed or the wording is vague (for example, “some documentation is missing”). | Every missing item is named specifically and the PM is told what to do next. |
| Edge case judgment | When the input was ambiguous (duplicate name, special characters, a rename), did Juno ask or choose something sensible? | 1 to 5 | Juno guessed and created the wrong or a duplicate record without saying so. | Juno handled it, but silently, so the PM wouldn’t know a judgment call was made. | Juno asked, or chose the safe option and told the PM what it chose and why. |
| Ready without edits | Would a PM leave the result as it is? | 1 to 5 | The PM would need to redo most of it. | The PM would make one or two small fixes. | The PM would leave it as is. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** All 8 demo initiatives after every change to the skill file, plus 5 more runs a week, including the 2 or 3 where I felt least sure.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus PM from my team, scoring independently before we compare.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on a scale question, or we split on a yes/no, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time. A split on a deal-breaker counts as a fail until we agree.

## Pass bar

Both yes/no questions pass, and the average is 4 or higher on each scale question (gap flagging, edge-case judgment, ready without edits).

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Consistency: any "no"
- Faithfulness: any "no"
- Juno creating or reporting a record that doesn’t exist in any source, or creating a duplicate initiative without telling the PM.
